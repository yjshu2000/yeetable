Icon composer

Tiles are at their true sizes — a 1 is 24px across, a 262144 is 168px. Drag one and it goes exactly where you put it; everything it touches gets shoved out of the way, and that shove carries on down the pile. Nothing coasts. The square frame sits underneath the tiles: drag it from any bare spot inside it, resize from a corner. Bury a corner under a tile and you'll have to nudge that tile aside to grab it again. Double-tap a tile to delete it. Everything saves as you go. Export SVG maps whatever the frame holds onto a 512 viewBox and hands you the file.
